**A powerful upgradable mining tool that evolves as you use it.**

Mine blocks to gain experience and unlock new levels. Each level increases the tool's power, durability, and abilities.

### ⚠️ Issues & Support
Found a bug or having an issue? Please contact me on Discord: **bassalo_**

## **Progression**

The Ultimate Tool has 6 levels.

As you mine, your tool progresses and unlocks:
- Increasing Efficiency
- Increasing Fortune
- Increasing durability
- Haste at higher levels
- Area mining at levels 5 and 6
- The Ultimate ability at level 6

## **Area Mining**
At level 5, the tool unlocks 3×3 mining.

At level 6, you can switch between:
- 3×3
- 5×5

Use:
/ultimatearea

to switch between the two modes.

## **Ultimate**
At level 6, hold Shift + Left Click to activate the Ultimate.

The Ultimate:
- Creates a massive destruction sphere
- Has a 10-block radius
- Costs 5000 durability
- Has a 5-minute cooldown
- Only destroys blocks included in the configured whitelist

## **Repair Cores**
Two Repair Cores are available:
- Repair Core 25%: restores 25% of the tool's maximum durability
- Repair Core 100%: fully repairs the tool

## **Configuration**
The blocks affected by the Ultimate Tool can be configured through the mod's common configuration.
Only whitelisted blocks can be mined by the tool.

## **Compatibility**
